<h3>Credit Card Purchases</h3>
<br/>
<div style="font-size:0.8em">
As of September 1st, 2026 a bank fee will be added to each credit card purchase.
<br/>
<br/>
<b>$2</b> will be added for purchases of <b>$1-$100</b>
<br/>
<b>$4</b> will be added for purchases of <b>$101-$200</b>
<br/>
<b>$6</b> will be added for purchases of <b>$201-$300</b>
<br/>
<br/>
Another <b>$2</b> will be added per every <b>$100</b> increase.

<br/>
<br/>
To avoid this extra charge we encourage cash or checks.

<br/>
<br/>

Did you know that the money you spend at our nursery goes not only to support our nursery work but also to fund the programs that our chapter offers? We fund up to $12,000 annually in scholarships for our local botany students. We put on a monthly program for the community. We offer free consultations for community members interested in starting or improving their own native plant garden. We feature an annual Wildflower show with educational presentations, art nights and knowledgeable people to answer your plant questions. We offer free field trips to explore our native plants.

<br/>
<br/>
And one of our missions is to keep our plant prices affordable so more native plants get spread out into the world.
